Privacy Policy

How Spendly collects, uses and protects your information

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data you enter: amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Basic technical data, such as browser type and log information, used to keep the service running.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To store your expenses and show you summaries and breakdowns.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve Spendly.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal or financial data. We share information only when required by law or to protect the security of the service.

4. Data security

Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies and sessions

Spendly uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use cookies for advertising.

6. Your choices

  • You can view, edit or delete your expenses at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data.

7. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When an account is deleted, its data is removed.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Spendly after changes means you accept the updated policy.
