  step-by-step teaching scaffold, and the repo name spindly is a misspelling of
  it. The tagline is "Track Every Rupee", and the amounts are in ₹.

  What exists now
  - app.py has working routes for the landing page, /register, and /login. Those
    routes only render templates, with no form handling yet.
  - The templates are base.html, landing.html, login.html, and register.html,
    plus CSS and JS in static/.
  - The landing page is a marketing page with a mock spending chart. It lists
    three planned features: quick expense logging, category and monthly
    breakdowns, and filtering by date range.

  What's still a stub
  - database/db.py is only a comment block. It describes get_db() (a SQLite
    connection with foreign keys on), init_db() (creates the tables), and
    seed_db() (adds sample data). This is "Step 1".
  - These routes return placeholder text:
    - /logout (Step 3)
    - /profile (Step 4)
    - /expenses/add (Step 7)
    - /expenses/<id>/edit (Step 8)
    - /expenses/<id>/delete (Step 9)

  So the project follows a numbered build plan, and Step 1 is the natural place
  to start: the SQLite setup in database/db.py. Steps 2 and 3 are presumably
  registration and login/logout. Want me to start on Step 1?
